Asthma
Overview:
Asthma is a chronic lung condition that causes inflammation and narrowing of the airways, making breathing difficult. It can affect people of all ages and may vary in severity over time.
Symptoms:
Common symptoms include coughing (especially at night), wheezing, shortness of breath, and chest tightness. Symptoms may worsen during exercise, at night, or when exposed to triggers.
Causes and Triggers:
Asthma can be influenced by genetic factors and environmental triggers such as dust, smoke, pollen, animal fur, air pollution, cold weather, respiratory infections, and strong odors.
Risk Factors:
People with a family history of asthma, allergies, obesity, exposure to tobacco smoke, or early-life respiratory infections are at higher risk.
Prevention:
Asthma symptoms can be reduced by:
* Avoiding known triggers and allergens
* Keeping living areas clean and well-ventilated
* Avoiding smoke and air pollution
* Following medical advice for asthma management
