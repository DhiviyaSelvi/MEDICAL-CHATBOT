
Diabetes
Overview:
Diabetes is a chronic condition that occurs when the body does not produce enough insulin or cannot use insulin effectively. Insulin helps regulate blood sugar levels, and uncontrolled diabetes can lead to serious health problems over time.
Symptoms:
Common symptoms include excessive thirst, frequent urination, fatigue, blurred vision, and unexplained weight loss. Symptoms may develop gradually, especially in type 2 diabetes.
Types of Diabetes:
* Type 1 Diabetes: Caused by little or no insulin production and requires lifelong insulin management.
* Type 2 Diabetes: Occurs when the body becomes resistant to insulin; it is often linked to lifestyle factors.
* Gestational Diabetes: Develops during pregnancy and usually resolves after childbirth.
Causes:
Diabetes can result from insulin resistance, autoimmune reactions, genetic factors, hormonal changes, and lifestyle-related factors such as obesity and lack of physical activity.
Prevention:
Type 2 diabetes can often be prevented or delayed through healthy lifestyle choices such as regular physical activity, balanced diet, maintaining a healthy weight, and avoiding tobacco use.
Complications:
If not managed properly, diabetes can affect the heart, kidneys, eyes, nerves, and blood vessels.

Prevention and Lifestyle (Diabetes)
Lifestyle changes play an important role in preventing or delaying type 2 diabetes. Healthy daily habits can also reduce the risk of long-term complications.
Key Prevention Measures:
* Maintain a healthy body weight.
* Engage in regular physical activity such as walking, cycling, or swimming.
* Eat a balanced diet rich in fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and legumes.
* Reduce intake of sugary foods, refined carbohydrates, and processed foods.
* Choose healthy fats such as those from nuts, seeds, fish, and vegetable oils.
* Avoid smoking and limit alcohol consumption.
* Reduce prolonged sitting and stay physically active throughout the day.
Adopting healthy lifestyle habits early can significantly lower the risk of developing type 2 diabetes and improve overall well-being.
