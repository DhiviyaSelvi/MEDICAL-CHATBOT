Dengue Fever
Overview:
Dengue fever is a viral infection transmitted to humans through the bite of infected mosquitoes. It is common in tropical and subtropical regions and can range from mild illness to severe disease.
Symptoms:
Many people with dengue may have mild or no symptoms. When symptoms occur, they may include high fever, headache, pain behind the eyes, muscle and joint pain, nausea, vomiting, and skin rash.
Causes:
Dengue is caused by dengue viruses and is spread through mosquito bites. A person cannot get dengue directly from another person.
Risk Factors:
People living in or traveling to tropical regions and individuals who have had dengue before are at a higher risk of infection and severe illness.
Prevention:
Dengue can be prevented by reducing mosquito exposure through measures such as:
* Wearing protective clothing
* Using mosquito repellents
* Using window screens and mosquito nets
* Eliminating stagnant water where mosquitoes breed
Complications:
In some cases, dengue can become severe and may cause serious health complications if not properly managed.
